- Five domains, about twenty assets each, overlapping at the edges (eg: a budget mentions recruitment, employment contract is legal and HR)
    - finance and reporting
    - people operations
    - product and engineering
    - marketing and sales
    - legal and compliance

- Noisy names: Untitled_3.pptx, IMG_4471.jpg, scan0012.pdf, etc. They carry no content.

- Four words are reserved and appear in no description anywhere: headcount, expansion, attrition, thermostat (a test enforces it)

- Same word meaning different things, eg:
    - retention	employee retention, customer retention, data retention
    - deck	a quarterly board deck, a wooden roof terrace deck
  
  Some of them should also appear only in filenames, never in a description, so that the queries have to be resolved on meaning alone

- Near duplicates, eg quarterly revenue Q1 and quarterly revenue Q2 (a query for the second quarter should rank Q2 above Q1)

- Mixed lengths (eg a long description covering multiple subjects)
